What are the odds the world is exactly as you think it is?

A weekly gathering in Flagstaff for impactful discourse.

What this is

Important Conversations with Fools is a social club that comes together to talk about what matters. Seek advice. Find support. Meet new people. Think deeply, and challenge what you believe about your own life and about the world.

Bring the thing you’ve never said out loud, or the thing about the world nobody seems willing to talk about. This is the place for it.

How much of what you believe did you ever check for yourself?

Why fools

To have a conversation you’ve never had, you have to be foolish enough to start it. To listen to all of it without judging, you have to be foolish enough to let go of being right.

The edges of the map

Everyone carries a map of the world, and every decision gets made by reading it. Old mapmakers drew monsters where their knowledge ran out. Most of us draw the coastline straight through.

These are the waters nobody has charted yet, not the experts and not you. Tap a monster.
